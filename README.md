## Bank of America Consumer-Complaint Monitoring and Response Analysis

# Project overview
This project analyze consumer complaints relating to Bank of America using Consumer Financial Protection Bureau(CFPB) complaint records from 2017 to 2023.

# Project objective
. Analyse the overall volume of consumer complaints
. Identify products and issues generating the highest complaint volume
. Examine company response to consumer complaint
. Evaluate timely and untimely response percentage
. Analyze monthly and yearly complaint trend
. Calculate the average and median CFPB forwarding interval

# Dataset description
. Source: Consumer Financial Protection Burea
. Company: Bank of America
. Period: May 2017 - August 2023
. Total records: 62516
. Columns: 12
